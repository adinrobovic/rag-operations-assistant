Server Overheating Procedure

If a server temperature exceeds the approved threshold, verify the temperature through the monitoring dashboard. Notify the operations lead and inspect airflow around the affected rack.

Do not immediately power down production equipment unless instructed by the incident-response lead.

All incidents must also be documented in the operations log.


Power Outage Procedure

During a power interruption, confirm whether backup generators and UPS systems activated successfully. Report any failed systems through the incident-management platform.


Visitor Access Procedure

All visitors must present identification and remain accompanied by an authorized employee while inside secure areas.
